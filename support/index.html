What to send with a bug report

  • Your shop domain.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • The page you were on, and the time it happened.
  • Any message the app showed you, copied exactly.

Common questions

Why is a product still a draft after publishing? Publishing is queued and runs in the background. The product page shows the sync status, and any error Shopify returned, as soon as Shopify answers.

Why did a price not change after the supplier raised its cost? Price automation holds a change that would break the maximum change or the minimum margin you set on the Automation page. The product’s change history says which guard held it.

Can Stocklane order from any supplier? No. Only suppliers with an integration can be ordered from automatically. Catalogues captured by hand are marked as manual: you place those orders on the supplier’s own site and record the shipment with “Fulfil manually”.

Cancelling

Uninstalling the app in Shopify ends the subscription and deletes the store’s data. Past Shopify orders are unaffected.
