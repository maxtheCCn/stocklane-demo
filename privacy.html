Privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

Stocklane is a Shopify app for dropshipping automation, operated by the app owner. This page explains what the app stores, why, and how a merchant can have it deleted.

What Stocklane stores

When you install the app on a Shopify store, it stores:

  • Your shop domain, store name, contact email, currency, time zone and the access scopes you granted.
  • The Shopify access token for your store, used to read and write the data you approved.
  • The name and email of the staff member who opens the app, so actions can be attributed in the activity log.
  • The products you import: titles, descriptions, images, prices, costs, stock levels and their link to the supplier listing.
  • Your orders: order number, line items, amounts, status, and the fulfilment and tracking records Stocklane creates.
  • Settings you choose, such as pricing rules, automation rules and notification preferences.
  • Supplier credentials you enter, encrypted at rest. They are never shown again after you save them.

Customer data

Stocklane does not request Shopify’s protected customer data access. Order webhooks therefore arrive without customer names, email addresses or shipping addresses unless your store has approved that access for this app, in which case those fields are stored only to pass a shipping address to the supplier that fulfils the order.

Stocklane never sells data, never uses it for advertising, and never shares it between merchants. Each store sees only its own data.

Who else processes the data

  • The hosting and database provider that runs this deployment.
  • Suppliers you connect: when you place a supplier order, the items and, if available, the shipping address are sent to that supplier so they can ship the order.

How long it is kept, and deletion

Data is kept while the app is installed. When you uninstall, Shopify sends a shop redaction request and Stocklane deletes the store and everything belonging to it, including sessions and access tokens. Customer redaction requests clear the customer name, email and shipping address from the affected orders. You can also ask for deletion at any time using the contact below.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the data held about your store, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion. Requests are answered within 30 days.
