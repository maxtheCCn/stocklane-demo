Add a supplier integration

Stocklane connects to suppliers through adapters that use each supplier’s official API.

An adapter implements product lookup, product details, variants, images, price, stock, order creation and tracking. Once registered, the supplier appears here with a secure credential form, and products, monitoring and orders work without other changes.

Stocklane only integrates suppliers through official APIs or permitted methods — never by scraping or bypassing protections.
